The team

Decades of regulatory expertise, delivered with personal attention.

Doreen Frank

President

Doreen Frank is an industry veteran with over 35 years of regulatory experience in the OTC and cosmetic industry in the US. She leads Envisage Consulting with a focus on practical, portfolio-wide regulatory strategy for clients navigating complex FDA requirements.

Doreen is an associate member of CHPA, the Personal Care Products Council, and the Independent Beauty Association. She also served for many years on the Executive Committee of the NY/NJ Regulatory Affairs Professional Society (RAPS) Chapter.

Kristin Sewell

Co-Owner

Kristin Sewell brings a wealth of experience and expertise in regulatory affairs, particularly in cosmetic and OTC drug labeling, cosmetic/drug listing (CDER Direct), and cosmetic formulation reviews. Her background includes artwork project management beginning at artwork development through production, working closely with graphic designers, printers, CMOs, and supply chain, including data systems, Esko, GS1, SmarterX, and WERCs.
