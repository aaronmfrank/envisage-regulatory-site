Our approach

Deep expertise. Tailored guidance.

Envisage Consulting, LLC is a woman-owned boutique firm offering specialized regulatory affairs consulting. We assist clients with marketed nonprescription drugs, cosmetic products, medical devices, and dietary supplements — and provide regulatory support for product development programs and Rx-to-OTC switch programs.

As a boutique firm, we provide personalized, dedicated attention — leveraging deep knowledge of FDA regulations including DSHEA, MoCRA, OTC monograph modernization, and 21 CFR requirements.

What we do

Four pillars of regulatory expertise

End-to-end regulatory support across every stage of the product lifecycle — from strategy and submissions through compliance and due diligence.

Regulatory strategy

Tailored regulatory strategies across OTC drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, and dietary supplements — including Rx-to-OTC switches, 505(b)(2) pathways, and monograph modernization.

Compliance & labeling

MoCRA compliance, Drug Facts panels, DSHEA labeling, EPR requirements, packaging artwork lifecycle management, and ongoing monitoring of state regulatory developments.

Comprehensive support

End-to-end submission support through OMORs, NDI notifications, cosmetic product listings, GMP guidance, adverse event reporting, and lifecycle management.

Due diligence & assessments

Due diligence reviews for acquisitions and licensing opportunities, regulatory assessments, ingredient evaluations, and pathway determinations.
