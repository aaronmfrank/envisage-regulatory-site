Our approach

Deep expertise. Tailored guidance.

Envisage Consulting, LLC is a woman-owned boutique firm offering specialized regulatory affairs consulting. We assist clients with marketed nonprescription drugs, cosmetic products, medical devices, and dietary supplements — and provide regulatory support for product development programs including Rx-to-OTC switch programs.

As a boutique firm, we provide personalized, dedicated attention — leveraging deep knowledge of FDA regulations including OTC monographs, DSHEA, MoCRA, OTC monograph modernization, and 21 CFR requirements.

What we do

Four pillars of regulatory expertise

End-to-end regulatory support across every stage of the product lifecycle — from strategy and submissions through compliance.

Regulatory strategy

Regulatory strategies for OTC drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, and dietary supplements — including Rx-to-OTC switches, 505(b)(2) pathways, 510(k) submissions, IND/NDA support, and OTC monograph, including OMOR submissions.

Compliance & labeling

Drug Facts / Supplement Facts labeling, monograph compliance, claims and advertising review.

Comprehensive support

End-to-end submission support for NDAs, INDs, OMORs, CDER Direct Registrations, cosmetic product listing.

Due diligence & assessments

Due diligence for acquisitions, M&A, and licensing; regulatory pathway assessments; ingredient safety and claims evaluation.
